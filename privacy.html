PocketPC Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 — version 1.0.0 (98) and later

PocketPC turns your iPhone into a desktop computer on an external display. It is built so your information stays with you. The developer does not run any servers for PocketPC and does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information.

What PocketPC collects

Nothing. PocketPC has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The developer does not receive the content you create or submit in PocketPC. Content you choose to send to a third-party AI service in AI Chats goes directly to that service, only after you allow it, as described below.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Websites and sign-ins. Pages you open, cookies, website data and browsing history are stored on your iPhone. PocketPC also keeps a protected, local copy of website session cookies to preserve sign-ins between launches; this copy is excluded from device backups. Website sign-out and Clear Website Data remove the affected saved sessions. iOS Passwords can fill saved credentials. PocketPC handles text entered through its keyboard, including passwords, transiently on your iPhone to forward it to the website you selected. It does not save website passwords or send them to the developer.
  • AI Notes and Live Translation (microphone and speech recognition). Speech is transcribed and translated on your iPhone. AI Notes summaries are written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Audio, transcripts, summaries and translations are not uploaded and are not sent to any third-party AI service. Notes are saved where you choose, such as Apple Notes or the Files app.
  • Location. Used only for walking directions in Smart Glasses. Route searches use Apple Maps. Location updates continue during active navigation while the iPhone is locked and stop when navigation ends. Build 96 removed native weather requests and does not send location to a weather provider.
  • Camera and microphone for websites. Only when a website you choose, such as a video-call site, asks and you allow it.
  • Local network. Used to connect Terminal (SSH) to computers you enter.
  • Terminal. Host details you save are kept on your iPhone. If you choose to remember a Terminal password, it is stored in this iPhone's Keychain. It is sent only to the SSH host you choose over the encrypted connection.
  • Diagnostics. Technical diagnostics are kept locally to help troubleshoot the app. They are not sent to the developer automatically. If you choose Share diagnostics, you choose the recipient and should review the file before sharing it.

AI Chats and third-party AI services

AI Chats is a window that opens the official websites of third-party AI chat services. These services are run by other companies, not by PocketPC:

  • ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) — OpenAI · OpenAI privacy policy
  • Claude (claude.ai) — Anthropic · Anthropic privacy policy
  • Gemini (gemini.google.com) — Google · Google privacy policy
  • Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com) — Microsoft · Microsoft privacy policy
  • Perplexity (perplexity.ai) — Perplexity AI · Perplexity AI privacy policy
  • Grok (grok.com) — xAI · xAI privacy policy
  • DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com) — DeepSeek · DeepSeek privacy policy
  • Le Chat (chat.mistral.ai) — Mistral AI · Mistral AI privacy policy
  • Meta AI (meta.ai) — Meta · Meta privacy policy
  • Muse (muse.ai) — Meta · Meta privacy policy
  • Any other chat website you add by address — the operator of that website

We ask first. PocketPC does not load a service or send it anything until you allow it. Before a service opens for the first time, PocketPC shows who runs it and what it will receive, and asks for your permission. If you choose Not Now, nothing is loaded and nothing is sent. Permission is given separately for each service.

What the service receives, and how. Once you allow a service and use it, your iPhone sends directly to that company, over an encrypted connection to its website:

  • everything you type, paste or dictate into the chat, and any files, photos or screenshots you attach;
  • your account details, if you sign in to the service;
  • standard web information that any website receives: your IP address, device and browser type, and the cookies that website sets.

How it is used. PocketPC uses this data only to show you the service you chose. PocketPC does not send your conversations to the developer, store them on any server, or share them with anyone else. Each AI company handles the data it receives under its own terms and privacy policy, which may include using it to provide, secure and improve its service. Please read the privacy policy of each service, linked above, before you use it.

Protection of your data. PocketPC does not provide your information to these companies on its own; they receive only what you choose to send them through their own websites. The developer has no agreement with these companies and receives no data from them.

Withdrawing permission. Open AI Chat Permissions in desktop Settings or in iPhone Controls to see which services are allowed and to withdraw permission. Withdrawing closes those chats, and PocketPC asks again before opening them. To also remove their sign-ins and cookies from your iPhone, use Clear Website Data. To delete conversations or your account with a service, use that service's own settings.

Third-party websites

PocketPC is a web browser. Websites you visit, including those in the Dock, are run by their owners and have their own privacy policies. The start page can load site icons from bookmarked websites. Its Technology News link opens Google News only when you choose it; PocketPC does not retrieve or reformat a news feed. These network services receive standard connection information, such as your IP address. Text, files, audio or other content you submit to a website are handled by that website under its own policy. PocketPC is not affiliated with those services.

Children

PocketPC gives unrestricted access to the web and is not intended for children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Controls > Clear Website Data, or the same control in desktop Settings, to close webpages and remove cookies, cached website content and saved website sessions. Favorites and downloaded files remain. You can delete notes and files from Files. Removing PocketPC deletes its app-container data, but copies you exported to Apple Notes or elsewhere remain in those destinations. iOS Keychain items can persist after uninstall; to remove a saved Terminal password, turn off Keep password in this iPhone's Keychain for that connection and reconnect.
