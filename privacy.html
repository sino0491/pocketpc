PocketPC Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 — build 96 and later

PocketPC turns your iPhone into a desktop computer on an external display. It is built so your information stays with you. The developer does not run any servers for PocketPC and does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information.

What PocketPC collects

Nothing. PocketPC has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Websites and sign-ins. Pages you open, cookies, website data and browsing history are stored on your iPhone. PocketPC also keeps a protected, local copy of website session cookies to preserve sign-ins between launches; this copy is excluded from device backups. Website sign-out and Clear Website Data remove the affected saved sessions. iOS Passwords can fill saved credentials. PocketPC handles text entered through its keyboard, including passwords, transiently on your iPhone to forward it to the website you selected. It does not save website passwords or send them to the developer.
  • AI Notes and Live Translation (microphone and speech recognition). Speech is transcribed and translated on your iPhone. Audio, transcripts and translations are not uploaded. Notes are saved where you choose, such as Apple Notes or the Files app.
  • Location. Used only for walking directions in Smart Glasses. Route searches use Apple Maps. Location updates continue during active navigation while the iPhone is locked and stop when navigation ends. Build 96 removed native weather requests and does not send location to a weather provider.
  • Camera and microphone for websites. Only when a website you choose, such as a video-call site, asks and you allow it.
  • Local network. Used to connect Terminal (SSH) to computers you enter.
  • Terminal. Host details you save are kept on your iPhone. If you choose to remember a Terminal password, it is stored in this iPhone's Keychain. It is sent only to the SSH host you choose over the encrypted connection.
  • Diagnostics. Technical diagnostics are kept locally to help troubleshoot the app. They are not sent to the developer automatically. If you choose Share diagnostics, you choose the recipient and should review the file before sharing it.

Third-party websites

PocketPC is a web browser. Websites you visit, including those in the Dock and AI Chats, are run by their owners and have their own privacy policies. The start page retrieves public technology headlines from Google News and site icons from bookmarked websites. These network services receive standard connection information, such as your IP address. Text, files, audio or other content you submit to a website are handled by that website under its own policy. PocketPC is not affiliated with those services.

Children

PocketPC gives unrestricted access to the web and is not intended for children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Controls > Clear Website Data, or the same control in desktop Settings, to close webpages and remove cookies, cached website content and saved website sessions. Favorites and downloaded files remain. You can delete notes and files from Files. Removing PocketPC deletes its app-container data, but copies you exported to Apple Notes or elsewhere remain in those destinations. iOS Keychain items can persist after uninstall; to remove a saved Terminal password, turn off Keep password in this iPhone's Keychain for that connection and reconnect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
