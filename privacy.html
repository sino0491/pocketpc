PocketPC Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

PocketPC turns your iPhone into a desktop computer on an external display. It is built so your information stays with you. The developer does not run any servers for PocketPC and does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information.

What PocketPC collects

Nothing. PocketPC has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Websites and sign-ins. Pages you open, cookies, website data and history are stored on your iPhone by Apple's WebKit, like in Safari. When you sign in to a website, your username and password go directly to that website. Saved passwords are filled by iOS Passwords with Face ID; PocketPC never sees or stores them.
  • AI Notes (microphone and speech recognition). Recordings are transcribed on your iPhone. Audio and transcripts are not uploaded. Notes are saved where you choose, such as Apple Notes or the Files app.
  • Location. Used only to show walking directions in Walk mode and local weather on the start page. Directions use Apple Maps. For weather, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch the forecast; nothing identifies you, and PocketPC stores nothing about the request.
  • Camera and microphone for websites. Only when a website you choose, such as a video-call site, asks and you allow it.
  • Local network. Used to connect Terminal (SSH) to computers you enter and to show the desktop on a Google TV or Chromecast you pick.
  • Terminal. Host details you save are kept on your iPhone; passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Third-party websites

PocketPC is a web browser. The websites you visit, including those in the dock, are run by their owners and have their own privacy policies. PocketPC is not affiliated with those websites.

Children

PocketPC gives unrestricted access to the web and is rated for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your iPhone, deleting PocketPC removes its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
