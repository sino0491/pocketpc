Show the desktop on a display

  • AirPlay: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and pick your Apple TV, AirPlay-compatible TV or Mac. The AirPlay button in PocketPC shows these steps. Tip: set your iPhone's Action Button to the Screen Mirroring control (Settings › Action Button › Controls) to connect with one press.
  • Cable: connect a USB-C display or adapter. The desktop appears automatically at the display's best resolution.
  • Google TV or Chromecast: open Controls and choose Cast to Google TV.

Use your iPhone as trackpad and keyboard

  • Move with one finger, tap to click, and scroll with two fingers.
  • Click with two fingers for Copy and Paste.
  • Tap Keyboard to type into the desktop. Bluetooth keyboards and mice work too.

Sound

Tap Sound to choose where audio plays, such as AirPods, a HomePod, or the display's speakers. PocketPC keeps your choice, and the iPhone's volume buttons control it. On a display connected by cable, use the Display volume slider on the iPhone.

AI Chats

Open AI Chats from the dock to keep your web-based AI assistants in one window. Sign in on each service's own website; add or remove services with Add a chat. PocketPC is not affiliated with these services.

Signing in to websites

Sign in to any website as you would in Safari. Saved passwords from iOS Passwords are filled with Face ID. Your passwords go directly to the website.

Smart Glasses

Tap Smart Glasses to turn your display into a see-through heads-up display for display glasses, then choose a mode:

  • Walking Directions: type a destination and pick a suggestion.
  • Live Translation: tap Start Translating to read a conversation in your language. Choose the language on that screen or in Controls. Translation happens on your iPhone; the first time you use a new language, iOS downloads it once.
  • AI Notes: Start Recording shows live captions, speakers and topics. Stop Recording saves the note in Files and Apple Notes.

Tap Smart Glasses again to return to the desktop.
