PocketPC Support

Your iPhone as a desktop computer.

Need help? Email sino0491@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

Show the desktop on a display

  • AirPlay: tap the AirPlay button in PocketPC, then Choose Display, and pick your Apple TV, AirPlay-compatible TV or Mac. You can also start Screen Mirroring from Control Center.
  • Cable: connect a USB-C display or adapter. The desktop appears automatically at the display's best resolution.
  • Google TV or Chromecast: open Controls and choose Cast to Google TV.

Use your iPhone as trackpad and keyboard

  • Move with one finger, tap to click, and scroll with two fingers.
  • Click with two fingers for Copy and Paste.
  • Tap Keyboard to type into the desktop. Bluetooth keyboards and mice work too.

Sound

Tap Sound to choose where audio plays, such as AirPods, a HomePod, or the display's speakers. PocketPC keeps your choice.

Signing in to websites

Sign in to any website as you would in Safari. Saved passwords from iOS Passwords are filled with Face ID. Your passwords go directly to the website.

Walk mode

Tap Walk, type a destination and pick a suggestion to get walking directions on your display. Tap Walk again to return to the desktop.

Privacy
